# From the client, Jeffrey


The basic idea is that this will be able to rotate 180° around the center "O". Its not something you'll look at cold and recognize it as the word "Motoproponent". Its more like something that when youre watching a video or some Motoproponent generated posts or media, and already have knowledge that this is a Motoproponent thing you'll see that the gliph looking thing does actually spell out Motoproponent.

Its a long way to go for an ambigram that isnt based on Ye Old English Script, like most. I had it drawn up a few years ago, like 15 years ago But have long since lost that paper. This is me trying to recreate it by explaining it to Devynn while she drew it out in Procreate on her iPad.

Its more squished in than it needs to be and all the circles (in the O's, P's and R's) are a little too oblong.

The three things that kinda made it "work" in the original was the top bar of the last "T" that makes up part of the first "M" extended all the way across and made an underline  and top border of the logo.

Then there is to be a color center line through it that goes from the center of the first "O"  (MOTO) to become the middle bar of the "E" (NENT). As the color line passes through it becomes the top bar of the second "T" (MOTO)

The main motivation for the ambigram is to be able to paint it on a helmet as the center stripe and it be the same from either side of my helmet. And now that Im probably going to get one of those cool helmets with the chinbar that flips all the way over.....
