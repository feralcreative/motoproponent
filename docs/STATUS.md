## Logo — the state of play

Latest: `assets/logos/motoproponent-lowercase-study-003.svg`, from `motoproponent-lowercase-generator.py`.

Study 003 applied four changes agreed with Ziad:

1. **50/50 glyph optimisation.** Studies 000–002 drew each glyph to read its best right way up, which produced a crisp `motopro` followed by mush. For an object read from both sides of a helmet that means a good side and a bad side. Each glyph now sits nearer the middle, so the whole word is evenly semi-legible.
2. **Heavier stroke** — `W_MAIN` 15 → 22. Paint, vinyl, embroidery and a 32px favicon all want mass.
3. **The rules are owned by a letter.** They were free-standing horizontals, which is why they caged the mark. The `m` now has a foot bar and that bar is what extends; rotated, it is the final `t`'s crossbar. They also stop past the pivot rather than closing a box.
4. **Tier two: the pivot-`o` roundel.** Thirteen symmetric letters is roughly 13:1 — fine as a helmet stripe, useless as an avatar or favicon. The roundel still reads at 32px.

### Open design decisions

- **Is the r/p compromise acceptable?** That pair is geometrically impossible (handedness is not preserved by 180° rotation) and is currently drawn to favour the `r`. Ziad has not signed off.
- **Heavier or lighter variant of 003?** Offered, not yet requested.
- **Mitre spikes at the `m`'s apex** — carried over from the uppercase studies, never resolved. Probably moot now.

### Correction on the record

An earlier draft claimed lowercase *solved* the o↔n pair. It does not — the notch width has no winning value (15px closes optically and the `n` reverts to an `o`; 34px turns the `o` into a `u` and the half read `mutupru`). It is a compromise at 20px, helped more by giving the bowl straight sides than by tuning the notch. The generator docstring and the study sheet notes were corrected; do not reintroduce the stronger claim.
