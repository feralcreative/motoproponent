# What goes public — decisions for Jeffrey

This is a set of questions, not a spec. Your answers decide three things: what the About page says, which accounts get publicly tied to the brand, and how search engines and AI assistants understand what Motoproponent *is*.

None of it is urgent and none of it blocks the site going up. But a few of these are annoying to reverse once they are indexed and cached, so they are worth a few minutes now.

Answer inline, however briefly. "Don't care" is a valid answer to most of them.

## 1. Is Motoproponent a brand, or is it you

The single most consequential question here.

- **A brand you happen to run.** Motoproponent is the thing; you are its founder. It can outlive your involvement, be handed to someone else, or be sold.
- **You, under a name.** Motoproponent is inseparable from Jeffrey. The channel is you riding, and there is no meaningful brand without you in it.

Either is fine, and it does not change how anything looks. It changes how the site describes itself in machine-readable terms, and going from "brand" to "person" later is much easier than the reverse.

## Notes

- **The share image is the exception.** Slack, iMessage, LinkedIn and Discord cache it hard and several never refresh, so whatever exists the first time the link gets pasted anywhere tends to stick.
